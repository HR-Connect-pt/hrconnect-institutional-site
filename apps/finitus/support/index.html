Finitus — Support

App version 1.3.0 · Last updated: September 21, 2026

Finitus is a life-progress tracker built around the philosophy of Memento Mori. It shows the weeks you have lived and the time you have left, based on your date of birth and your life expectancy, on your phone and on your home screen widget.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set or change my date of birth and life expectancy?

Open Settings from the main screen, answer “When is your birthday?” and “What is your life expectancy?”, then tap Save.

How do I add the widget to my home screen?

Finitus includes a step-by-step tutorial: tap “Learn how to add widgets” on the main screen. In short:

  • iPhone and iPad — touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit, choose Add Widget, search for Finitus, pick a size and tap Add Widget.
  • Android — touch and hold an empty area of the home screen or the Finitus icon, tap Widgets, find the Finitus widget, then drag it where you want it.

Three sizes are available: small, medium and large.

My widget is not showing the right numbers.

The operating system decides when a widget refreshes, so a change can take a few minutes to appear.

What is Finitus Pro and what does the “Notify me” button do?

Finitus Pro is a future version of the app. Tapping “Notify me” only puts you on the list for a single notification when Pro is released.

Which data does the app collect?

Your date of birth and your life expectancy are stored only on your device and never leave it.

Which languages are supported?

English, Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil), Spanish, French, German and Italian.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Write to the support email above. Every message is read by the team that builds the app.
